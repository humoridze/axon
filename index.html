Razer mice in the browser

Sets DPI, polling rate, and lighting, then writes it to the mouse. No desktop app.

  • Chrome or Edge, mouse over USB
  • Fully quit Razer Synapse, including the tray
  • In the HID dialog pick a Razer device, not “mouse”
